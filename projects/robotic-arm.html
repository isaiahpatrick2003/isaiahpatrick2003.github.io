Why this project

Learn mechanical design by building something that has to move

The project is deliberately centered on a gap in my current skill set: mechanical CAD and mechatronic design. Rather than learning SolidWorks through isolated tutorials, I want to use it to solve real problems involving moving joints, load paths, clearances, mounting, assembly, and iteration.

Version 1 scope

Start simple, then earn more axes

The first version is planned around base rotation, shoulder, elbow, and a gripper. Wrist axes and more advanced control can be added in later revisions after the basic mechanical system works reliably.

Hobby robotic-arm projects are being studied for scope and common mechanical approaches, while the parts and assembly will be independently designed.

Development path

What This Project Is Intended to Teach

01

Mechanical CAD

Sketch constraints, dimensions, parts, assemblies, mates, interference, clearances, and design for 3D printing in SolidWorks.

02

Joint Design

Bearings, shafts, fasteners, servo mounting, load transfer, printed tolerances, cable routing, and assembly order.

03

Embedded Control

Servo control and motion sequencing with an Arduino first, leaving room for a more capable controller or custom electronics later.

04

Robotics Fundamentals

Use the physical arm to learn coordinate frames, forward and inverse kinematics, motion limits, repeatability, and eventually closed-loop improvements.

Current progress

Early SolidWorks Work

Where It Stands

  • Version 1 scope has been narrowed to a small four-axis arm.
  • Initial base sketches and geometry are underway in SolidWorks.
  • The early CAD work is focused on learning the workflow while thinking through reach, joint layout, mounting, and printable construction.

Physical Prototyping

The project is still early and progress is intentionally flexible. The next major step will be turning the first CAD concepts into printable parts and evaluating them physically once regular 3D-printing access is available.

Because this is a personal project, updates will be added when meaningful design or prototype milestones are reached rather than on a fixed schedule.
