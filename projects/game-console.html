Project direction

From accelerator concept to a complete physical system

The project began with an FPGA / hardware-accelerator direction, then pivoted toward a handheld game console that could still demonstrate meaningful hardware and embedded engineering while producing a complete, tangible device.

The current direction is a small custom console built around a microcontroller or high-performance MCU/SoC with C/C++ firmware, a graphical display, physical controls, audio, battery operation, and eventually a custom PCB and enclosure.

Engineering approach

Prototype first, then freeze the architecture

Display support, memory, audio, storage, wireless, power consumption, development tools, cost, and the difficulty of a final custom PCB all need to be considered together.

The team plans to prototype the major subsystems, measure real behavior, and use those results to guide the final integrated hardware.
